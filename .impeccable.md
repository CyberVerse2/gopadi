## Design Context

### Users

GoPadi serves two roles on the same surface in Nigerian university towns (Nsukka, Enugu, and similar markets):

- **Customers** — students, hostel residents, busy parents, market traders, age 18–35. They post errands from a phone, often outdoors in daylight, often on the move. They are anxious about handing money to a stranger who could disappear.
- **Padis** (runners) — same demographic, often working between classes or shifts. They accept errands and walk/ride to complete them. They are anxious about doing the work and not getting paid.

Both jobs-to-be-done collapse into one need: **hand off a small local task to a stranger and know the money is safe.** Escrow on Stellar via Trustless Work is the answer. The interface's only real job is to make the escrow feel real, present, and inevitable.

Primary device: cheap to mid-range Android phones. Primary network: spotty 3G/4G. Primary lighting: anything from market sun to a hostel room at 11pm.

### Brand Personality

Three words: **exact, deadpan, alive.**

The product is moving money. The voice is calm and exact — closer to a receipt or a courier waybill than a marketing surface. But it is not a bank: small Nigerian wit lives in microcopy, empty states, and error messages. The personality reads as: *honest tool, owned by people who know the streets.*

Emotions to evoke, in order of priority:
1. **Confidence the money is safe** — the dominant feeling
2. **Speed** — every interaction should feel like a single tap committed to a ledger
3. **Recognition** — "this was made for me, not translated from San Francisco"

### Aesthetic Direction

**Brutalist / utilitarian. Dark by default.**

The interface reads like a piece of well-made equipment — exposed structure, big honest type, generous monospace, a single hot accent color used scarcely. Hierarchy is built from weight, scale, and tabular numerals, not from cards or shadows. Borders are 1px hairlines or absent; the page is a structured void. Every pixel earns its place.

Phone-first. Mobile is the canonical view; desktop is the wide variant.

**Anti-references (explicit, non-negotiable):**
- The old GoPadi system — forest green + marigold orange, Manrope, rounded pills, "market board" framing. Discarded entirely.
- Generic crypto dapp aesthetics — neon-on-pure-black, glassmorphism, purple-to-cyan gradients, "Connect Wallet" as the visual centerpiece.
- Generic Stripe-clone SaaS — pastel rounded cards, friendly stock illustrations, "Built for teams" energy.
- African-stereotype visuals — mudcloth patterns, Adinkra symbols, kente borders, baobab silhouettes, sunset-over-savanna photography. Lazy shortcuts that we refuse.

**Theme:** Dark default. Near-black canvas (not pure black — tinted toward the brand hue), bone-white primary text (not pure white), single hot signal color reserved for action, state, and escrow emphasis. Light variant can come later but is not the design target.

### Design Principles

1. **The interface is equipment, not packaging.** Every element exists to do a job. Decoration that doesn't carry information is removed. Borders, weight, scale, and one accent color do all the work — never illustration, never gradient, never stock visual.

2. **Money has a typeface.** USDC amounts, escrow IDs, Stellar addresses, fees, timestamps, and contract states are rendered in monospace at hero scale. The number is the headline of the page. The user looks at the figure first and the title second.

3. **Dark, but readable in market sun.** High contrast is the brutalist promise. Near-black surfaces against bone-white type, with the signal color reserved for moments where the user must look. No mid-gray copy on dark — text is either present (bone-white) or quiet (tinted neutral), never washed out.

4. **Two roles, one canonical truth.** Customer and Padi see the same escrow, the same state, the same numbers. Their available actions branch (post / accept / fund / prove / confirm / release / dispute), but the data is shared — the page is a single ledger view, not two role-specific dashboards.

5. **Wit lives in copy, not chrome.** Microcopy, empty states, errors, and confirmations may sound like a friend from Nsukka. Color, layout, motion, and components do not — they stay strictly functional, deadpan, undecorative.
