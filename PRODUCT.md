# GoPadi — Design Context

## Users
Customers and runners in Nigerian university towns (Nsukka, Enugu) and similar markets. They post and accept errands from phones during the day, often on the move. Cohort skews 18–35: students, hostel residents, busy parents, market traders. Trust is the central anxiety — both sides fear getting burned. Escrow is the answer.

## Brand Personality
**Friendly, trustworthy, confident.** Three words: *approachable, reliable, energetic.* Reads like a modern consumer fintech with West African warmth — not luxury editorial, not generic SaaS. Feels safe enough for your mother and modern enough for your cousin in school.

## Aesthetic Direction
**Bright, rounded, confident.** A bold geometric wordmark in two tones (forest green "Go" + marigold orange "Padi") sets the entire system. Everything else follows that energy: clean white surfaces with subtle warmth, big bold display headlines in rounded geometric sans, money as the hero in tabular numerals, generous spacing, asymmetric layouts. Orange is a true co-brand color — not a precious accent — used confidently alongside green throughout.

**Anti-references**: Editorial serif italics; museum-catalog restraint; pure black on stark white; cyan-to-purple gradients; glassmorphism; gradient text; dark dashboards with neon accents; the same-sized 4-card "How it works" template; tribal-pattern visual clichés.

## Design Principles
1. **The wordmark sets the tone.** Two-tone Go/Padi (green/orange) is the brand. Anywhere the brand name appears, that two-tone treatment shows up.
2. **Numbers are heroes.** USDC amounts, escrow totals, timeline steps — render them at confident scale in bold tabular sans.
3. **Bright but warm.** Off-white, not stark white. Saturated but never neon. Orange and green sit together as equals.
4. **Weight, not italic.** Emphasis comes from weight contrast (300 vs 800) and color (green ↔ orange), never serif italics.
5. **Asymmetry > grids.** Break centered, balanced layouts. Let hero elements breathe asymmetrically.
6. **Rounded but precise.** Geometric sans, generous corners on interactive surfaces, but exact alignment and crisp typographic detail.
