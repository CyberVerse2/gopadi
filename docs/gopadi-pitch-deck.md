# GoPadi 8-Page Pitch Deck

## 1. Cover

# GoPadi

**Local errands protected by escrow.**

GoPadi helps people hire nearby Runners to buy essentials, pick up items, or complete small errands, with payment protected through Trustless Work escrow.

**One-liner**

Post an errand. A trusted Padi accepts. Funds stay locked until the job is done.

## 2. Problem

Everyday errands are still messy, risky, and stressful.

People need help buying foodstuff, fuel, groceries, medicine, or handling local pickups. The first problem is discovery: where do they find nearby people or Runners who are actually available to help?

Even when they find someone, the second problem is trust. Can they trust that person with their money, their items, and the responsibility of completing the errand properly?

**Customers worry:**

- What if the Runner disappears with my money?
- What if they buy the wrong item?
- What if there is no proof?

**Runners worry:**

- What if I do the errand and the customer refuses to pay?
- What if I spend my time and transport money for nothing?

This trust gap makes simple local errands harder than they should be.

## 3. Solution

GoPadi creates a safer errand marketplace for customers and local Runners.

The flow is simple:

1. Customer posts an errand.
2. A Runner accepts it.
3. Customer funds escrow.
4. Runner completes the errand.
5. Runner uploads proof.
6. Customer confirms completion.
7. Funds are released.

If something goes wrong, the customer or Runner can open a dispute for review.

GoPadi makes errands feel safer because neither side has to rely on blind trust.

## 4. How We Used Trustless Work

GoPadi uses **Trustless Work single-release escrow** to protect every money-backed errand.

**Role mapping**

| GoPadi Role | Trustless Work Role |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Funder |
| Customer | Approver |
| Runner / Padi | Receiver |
| Admin | Dispute Resolver |
| GoPadi | Platform |

**Why it matters**

- The customer knows funds are not released until the task is completed.
- The Runner knows payment has already been locked before they start.
- If something goes wrong, the dispute flow allows a resolver to release or refund funds.

Trustless Work is the infrastructure layer that makes GoPadi's trust promise real.

## 6. Go To Market

**Beachhead**

Nigerian university towns and dense local communities.

**Initial target users**

- Students in hostels
- Busy workers
- Parents and families
- Market shoppers
- Local errand Runners looking for income

**Launch strategy**

- Start in high-density communities like Nsukka and Enugu.
- Recruit trusted first Runners manually.
- Seed common errands: foodstuff, fuel, groceries, medicine, package pickup.
- Use campus ambassadors and local WhatsApp groups.
- Build trust through visible proof, wallet-backed payments, and resolved dispute history.

After proving repeat usage in one local market, GoPadi can expand city by city.
